# Outreach Templates

**Date:** 2026-09-04

Three messages. Send 1 and 2 this week — both have reply lead time.

---

## 1. Operator footage request

Short by design. One ask, no attachment, no pitch. Send as an Instagram DM or to
the contact email on the operator's own site — not through the OTA.

> Subject: Featuring [Tour Name] on TikTok — quick footage ask
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I run a TikTok account covering things to do in NYC under $80, and I'm putting
> together a video on [Tour Name].
>
> Two quick questions:
>
> 1. Do you have any raw footage or photos I could use? Even phone clips work —
>    rough tends to perform better than polished.
> 2. If not, am I okay to use clips from your Instagram, with credit?
>
> The video tags your listing so viewers can book directly from it. No cost to
> you, and I'll send you the link when it's live so you can repost it.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

**Why it works:** the ask is small, the benefit is concrete and immediate, and
option 2 costs them nothing but a yes. Save every reply — an emailed "yes, go
ahead" is your written permission.

**Volume:** send 10-15 to get 5-8 usable yeses. Batch them; don't wait on replies.

---

## 2. Partner support — media usage rights

Send to Viator affiliate support and GetYourGuide partner support separately.
This answer decides whether the slideshow format is viable at all.

> Subject: Affiliate media usage rights — product images in social video
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm an affiliate partner (or applying to be one) and I want to confirm usage
> rights before building anything.
>
> Does affiliate access to the content endpoints permit me to use product images
> and supplier media in short-form social video — specifically TikTok posts that
> link back to the listing for booking?
>
> Three specifics:
>
> 1. Are supplier-provided images licensed for republication in social content,
>    or only for display on an affiliate's own site?
> 2. Does that change if the video is monetised through TikTok GO commissions
>    rather than a standard affiliate link?
> 3. Is attribution or a specific credit format required?
>
> Happy to work within whatever the terms are — I just want it in writing first.
>
> Thanks,
> [Name]

**Why it's worded this way:** it asks for a written answer, names the exact use
case, and pre-commits to compliance. Vague questions get vague answers you can't
rely on.

## 3. Paid service version — send to non-responders and to operators who said yes

The same operators, a different ask. This one pays in 30 days instead of 120.

> Subject: Short videos for [Tour Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I make short-form video for NYC tour and attraction operators — the vertical
> TikTok/Reels format, cut to trending audio.
>
> Most operators I talk to have great photos and no video, or video that was shot
> for a website five years ago and doesn't work on a phone.
>
> I'd do a batch of 4 videos for [Tour Name] for $[X]. You own them outright and
> can run them as ads or organic on any platform.
>
> Want me to send a sample cut using your existing photos, no charge? Takes me
> about an hour and you can decide from there.
>
> [Name]

**Why the free sample:** it removes the decision. You are building these videos
anyway for the affiliate side, so the marginal cost of showing one is near zero.

**Pricing:** anchor at $400-800 for a batch of 4, or a $600-1,200/mo retainer for
8-12/mo. Local operators pay this. Do not price against affiliate commission —
this is a service, and it settles on invoice terms, not on booking close.

# Tier 1 — Reach Concepts (AI-generated, untagged)

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Companion to:** `tiktok-go-templates.md` (Tier 2, the tagged/bookable videos)

Tier 1 exists to build the follower base that Tier 2 rides on. TikTok shows every
new video to your own followers first, and first-hour follower engagement gates
whether it climbs the distribution ladder. A larger, correctly-classified follower
base measurably improves how far the *monetised* videos travel.

**No POI tag. Nothing is sold.** So there is no venue-accuracy problem and no
misrepresentation risk. AIGC toggle on, always.

## The strategic frame

**AI's edge is impossibility, not realism.** Generic AI beauty shots lose to real
footage every time — there is no tension and no reason to watch past 0:03. Shots
that *cannot be filmed* have no competition.

This also dissolves the polished-vs-UGC problem. Authenticity is not the axis when
the premise is openly impossible.

## ⚠️ The trap that would sink this

**Tier 1 must be travel content that happens to be AI-made — never AI content that
happens to be about travel.**

The algorithm classifies your followers. If Tier 1 attracts an AI-art audience, it
will start serving your bookable videos to people who like AI renders and have no
intention of booking a tour. That is worse than posting nothing: it actively
degrades Tier 2 by poisoning the follower signal that gates its distribution.

Test every concept against: *would someone planning an NYC trip follow this?* If the
honest answer is "no, but someone who likes AI art would," cut it.

## C1 — NYC Through Time

Same frame, same angle, across eras.

```
0:00-0:02  Recognisable location, present day. Text: "Times Square, 1904"
0:02-0:12  Morph/cut through eras. 1904 -> 1920s -> 1950s -> 1980s -> today.
0:12-0:16  Land on present day. Text: "same corner, 120 years"
```

**Why it earns reach:** impossible to film, factually grounded, and the comments
write themselves — *"do the Brooklyn Bridge next"* is free engagement and free
content direction.
**Repeatability:** effectively infinite. Locations x eras.
**Travel-audience test:** passes. Anyone interested in visiting NYC cares about this.

## C2 — Unbuilt New York

Architectural proposals that were never constructed, rendered.

```
0:00-0:03  Text over a real skyline: "this was almost built in Manhattan"
0:03-0:12  The unbuilt design, rendered in place, slow push-in
0:12-0:18  Text: "cancelled in [year]. here's what's there now."
```

**Why it earns reach:** it is *true*, which is the strongest defence against the
inauthentic-content classifier. High save rate and high share rate simultaneously.
**Source material:** Hugh Ferriss renderings, cancelled skyscraper proposals, the
unbuilt Penn Station replacements. Real history, visualised.
**Travel-audience test:** passes.

---

## C3 — When To Actually Go

Same location across seasons and weather. Genuinely useful for trip planning.

```
0:00-0:02  Text: "when to visit Central Park"
0:02-0:14  Same frame: spring -> summer -> autumn -> snow. Hold each ~3s.
0:14-0:18  Text: "late October. it's not close."
```

**Why it earns reach:** answers a question people actually search. Opinionated
ending drives comments from people who disagree.
**Repeatability:** every park, bridge, street, and neighbourhood.
**Travel-audience test:** passes strongly — this is planning content.

## C4 — 6am vs 6pm

The same place, empty and mobbed.

```
0:00-0:02  Split or hard cut. Text: "6am"
0:02-0:08  Empty, quiet, golden
0:08-0:14  Cut. Text: "6pm" — packed, chaotic
0:14-0:18  Text: "go early. every time."
```

**Why it earns reach:** immediately legible, zero explanation needed, and the
practical takeaway makes it save-worthy.
**Bridges to Tier 2:** this is the reach version of T4 (The Correction) and trains
the audience for the tagged comparison videos.

## C5 — Neighbourhood Character

What each neighbourhood *feels* like, stylised rather than documented.

```
0:00-0:02  Text: "where to stay in NYC, by vibe"
0:02-0:14  3-4 neighbourhoods, ~3s each, each with a one-word label
0:14-0:18  Text: "which one is you?"
```

**Why it earns reach:** the closing question is engineered for comments, and
"where should I stay" is one of the highest-intent travel questions there is.
**Bridges to Tier 2:** viewers who answer are self-identifying as trip planners —
exactly the follower profile Tier 2 needs.

## Cadence and ratio

- **Early (while footage is still arriving):** Tier 1 heavy, roughly 70/30. It is
  what you can actually produce before operator replies land.
- **Steady state:** target 50/50. Do not let Tier 1 dominate indefinitely, or the
  account reads as an AI channel and the inauthentic-content policy applies.
- **Both tiers stay in one niche** (NYC travel), so the 80% niche rule is satisfied
  and format variety within it is unpenalised.
- **Series beat one-offs.** The 2026 algorithm rewards returning viewers. Number the
  entries and keep the frame identical across a series so it is recognisable.

## Do not log Tier 1 in golab

`golab` measures the conversion experiment. Only **tagged** videos count toward the
30 and toward the kill criterion. Logging untagged reach videos would inflate
`videosMeasured` and corrupt the verdict.

Track Tier 1 separately on follower growth and profile visits — its job is audience,
not bookings.

# NYC POI Shortlist

**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Source:** GetYourGuide NYC catalogue, pulled 2026-09-04. Prices and review
counts are GetYourGuide's, **not** TikTok GO commission rates.

## Selection criteria

1. **Under $80** — the conversion threshold. Over $80 drops below 1%.
2. **High review count** — proxy for booking volume. GO sets commission per POI by
   GMV, so high-volume POIs should pay higher rates.
3. **Visually distinctive** — must carry a 12-20s vertical video with no narration.

## Tier 1 — the observation deck cluster

Four decks, all $44-48, all 20k+ reviews. This is the strongest starting cluster on
the list and it comes with a format built in.

| POI | Price | Reviews | Rating | Visual note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SUMMIT One Vanderbilt** | $48 | **59,575** | 4.7 | Mirrored infinity rooms. The most TikTok-native attraction in NYC. |
| **Empire State Building** | $48 | 28,743 | 4.7 | Iconic, instantly legible in frame one |
| **Edge** | $44 | 23,222 | 4.6 | Angled glass deck + glass floor |
| **Top of the Rock** | $46 | 22,036 | 4.6 | The view *of* the Empire State |

**Why this cluster first:** it hands you T4 (The Correction) for free —
`"don't do the Empire State, do this instead"` is a real debate people already have,
with four interchangeable, similarly priced options. High volume, right price band,
maximum visual payoff, and the comparison writes its own hooks.

## Excluded and why

- **Helicopter tour** ($272), **Flying Dress Photoshoot** ($367), **Niagara day trip**
  ($161), **CityPASS** ($149), **dinner cruises** ($115-145) — all over $80.
- **Horse carriage** ($162/group) — over threshold, plus animal-welfare comment risk.
- **MJ the Musical** ($71) — in band, but filming inside is prohibited, so there is
  no footage path.
- **Sunset Cruise with Live DJ** ($20) — cheapest on the list but 3.6 stars on 62
  reviews. A bad experience produces refunds, and refunds claw back commission.

## Before shooting anything

1. **Verify each POI is actually in the TikTok GO catalogue** at
   `partner.tiktok-go.us`, and record its real commission rate. GetYourGuide
   popularity does not guarantee GO inclusion or a good rate.
2. **Check filming restrictions.** Observation decks generally permit personal
   filming; commercial use often needs written permission. Another reason the
   operator outreach comes first.
3. **Log the real commission into `golab`** as `poiCommissionUsd` — expected value
   per video is `commission x conversion`, and only the first half is knowable up
   front.
